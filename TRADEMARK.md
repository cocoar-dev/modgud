Ask first (see contact below) before you:

  on Modgud" in the description.
- Use the Modgud name or shield in a way that could suggest your
  product, service, or company is endorsed by, affiliated with, or

- Sell merchandise (T-shirts, stickers, etc.) carrying the Modgud
  name or logo.

## Logo use

If you use the shield logo in the "powered by" sense above:

- Don't modify it (colors, proportions, added elements).
- Don't combine it with your own product name in a way that creates a
  composite mark.
- Don't use it as your application's primary icon.
- Keep clear space around it — at least the height of the shield.

The SVG files in `docs/public/` are the canonical sources.

## Forks

You're welcome to fork — that's what the license is for. When you do:

- Rename the binary, container image, NuGet package, and any user-
  visible product strings.
- Replace the shield logo with your own.
- Keep the `NOTICE` and `LICENSE` files. You may add a line crediting
  Modgud as the upstream.
- Don't claim or imply COCOAR endorses your fork.

## Permission requests

Email **`bwi@cocoar.dev`** with a one-paragraph description of the
intended use and a sample if you have one. Common cases (conference
talks, comparison posts, integration partner badges) are usually
approved within a few days.
