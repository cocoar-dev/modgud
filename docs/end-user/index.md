# End-user help

Welcome to Modgud. This section helps you sign in, secure your account, and manage your profile.

If you've just been invited and need to set up your account for the first time, jump to [First steps](./first-steps).

::: tip Interface language
Modgud's screens default to German the first time you sign in. On the login page, use the small language toggle in the corner to switch to English for that session; once signed in, set it permanently under Profile → Preferences → Language & Region.
:::

## What is Modgud?

Modgud is an **identity provider**. Instead of a separate password for every connected app, you sign in here once and all linked apps know who you are (Single Sign-On).

Manage from this account:

- Your **sign-in methods** (password, 2FA, passkeys, external providers)
- Your **profile** (name, email, picture)
- Your **active sessions** (which devices are currently signed in)
- Your **privacy settings** (export data, delete account)
